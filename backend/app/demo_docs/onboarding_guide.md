# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

On day one, IT hands over your laptop and sets up your company email account. Bring a government photo ID for the access card.

Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team. Meet your buddy daily for the first two weeks.

Complete the security awareness training within your first 7 days. Access to production systems is granted only after this training is complete.

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager holds a review at the end of month three and month six.

Expense claims for travel are submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the trip, with receipts attached.
