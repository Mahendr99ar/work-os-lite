# Leave Policy

Every full-time employee gets 18 days of paid leave per calendar year. Leave is credited at the start of January.

Unused leave carries over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

Apply for planned leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request within three working days.

Sick leave is separate from paid leave. Employees get 8 sick days per year. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than two consecutive days.

During the notice period, paid leave can only be taken with written approval from the department head.
